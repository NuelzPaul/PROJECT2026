Seven days, one community

We share the food, music and stories we grew up with, and learn from each other, before the holidays begin.

What's on

Music & dance

Afrobeats, highlife, dancehall, soca and live drumming.

Food

Jollof, suya, jerk, rice and peas, sorrel and more.

Fashion

Ankara, kente, madras and Christmas-week style.

Stories

Anansi tales, poetry and memories shared by friends.

Three nights not to miss

18Fri Dec

Opening Night & LibationMain event

Honour our ancestors, then dance to live drumming.

19Sat Dec

Food FestivalMain event

Stalls, cooking demos and a crowd-favourite vote.

24Thu Dec

Grand Christmas Eve PartyMain event

Live performances, a shared feast and dancing late.

Plan your visit

Is it really free?

Yes. Every event is free. Please RSVP so we can plan food and space.

Do I need to be African or Caribbean to come?

No. The week is open to everyone.

Can I bring friends and family?

Yes. Tell us how many are coming when you RSVP.

What about dietary needs?

Contact us at least five days before. See the Meals page.

Hosted by the African & Caribbean Society

Roots & Rhythm is organised by our society, and everyone is invited to take part.
