Activities

Come to watch, join in or learn something new. No experience needed.

Music & dance

Live drumming and DJ sets covering Afrobeats, highlife, dancehall and soca.

  • Live drumming on Opening Night
  • Dance workshop on Wednesday 23 Dec
  • Amapiano dance floor every evening

Fashion

Wear your favourite traditional or festive outfit and join the showcase.

  • Ankara, kente and madras styles
  • Showcase on Sunday 20 Dec
  • Photo corner for everyone

Stories

Gather round for tales, poetry and memories of home and Christmas.

  • Anansi tales
  • Poetry readings
  • Abunuwasi tales
  • The Talking Drum(Ngoma)
  • Open floor for your own story

Football

Friendly matches for all ages and abilities.

  • Five-a-side on Tuesday 22 Dec
  • Teams formed on the day
  • Snacks and awards afterwards

Want to be on stage?

Dance, sing, drum or recite. Tell us about your act and we will build it into the programme.
